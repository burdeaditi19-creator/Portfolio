Process

A thoughtful approach to creating meaningful digital experiences. From understanding users to refining the final interface, each step brings clarity, creativity, and purpose to the design journey.

Discover

Understand the problem:

Research users, their needs, behaviors, and pain points to understand the context behind the problem. Explore existing experiences, identify gaps, and uncover opportunities.

01

Define

Set the direction:

Bring research insights together to clearly define the problem and user goals. Establish key requirements, priorities, and experience principles that create a strong foundation.

02

Develop

Design & iterate:

Turn ideas into wireframes, visual concepts, and interactive prototypes. Explore different solutions, test key interactions, gather feedback, and continuously refine.

03

Deliver

Create the final experience:

Transform the refined concepts into polished, intuitive interfaces with a consistent visual language. Focus on usability, accessibility, interaction details, and design consistency.

04

Frequently asked questions

My design process involves Discovery (understanding the problem), Definition (aligning goals), Development (prototyping and iterating), and Delivery (finalizing UI and handing off).

I primarily use Figma for UI design and prototyping. For user research and ideation, I utilize tools like FigJam, Miro, Notion, and various testing platforms.

Success is measured through user testing, tracking KPIs such as task success rate, reduced error rates, and increased conversion, ensuring the experience feels seamless.
